Which areas do you cover for home visits?

Home visits are primarily around Sushant Lok and nearby parts of Gurugram. Share your location on WhatsApp and Dipika will confirm availability.

Do I need any equipment?

No. Comfortable clothes and a little space are enough to begin. If a mat or other equipment would help your plan, Dipika will guide you.

Is yoga a medical treatment?

Yoga and movement sessions support wellbeing but are not a substitute for medical diagnosis or treatment. Please follow your doctor’s advice for any health condition.

Can you arrange corporate sessions?

Yes. Corporate yoga can be arranged on-site or virtually for offices and teams. Message with your team size and preferred format.

How do I book a session?

Tap any WhatsApp button on this page or call +91 70170 86313. We’ll discuss your goals, timing and the best place to start.
